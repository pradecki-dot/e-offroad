Parts and accessories

Spare parts and accessories for electric motorcycles, quads, go-karts and bikes. Below you will find the manufacturers whose parts and accessories we offer. Ask about availability in our showroom at ul. Grenadierów 7E in Kraków or write to info@e-offroad.com.

Manufacturers

Antelopower

Antelopower makes the LEAP ANTELOPE electric enduro motorcycle (LA25, 72 V, 25 kW peak power) as well as original parts and accessories for this model.

  • 72 V 60 Ah battery (4320 Wh), Samsung 50S NCM cells, UN38.3 certified, removable
  • 1200 W charger (72 V 15 A), IP67, 110–220 V, up to 80% in approx. 2.5 h
  • 72 V 300 A FOC controller
  • Chassis, suspension and brake spare parts (per the manufacturer’s parts catalogue)

See Antelopower parts (210 items) →

EWATT

EWATT makes electric off-road motorcycles. Our range includes the EWATT Beast-M, EWATT Beast-M (road-legal) and EWATT Beast XS for kids.

Arctic Leopard

Arctic Leopard (Zhejiang Arctic Leopard Electric Technology Co., Ltd.) is a Chinese maker of high-performance electric enduro and motocross bikes. The company designs its own motors, battery packs and frames. Its range includes the E-XE, E-X, X-E (XE PRO), X-F (XF PRO) and E-XT series, as well as models for kids.

  • Spare and service parts for the XE PRO and XF PRO models we sell
  • Batteries, chargers and drivetrain components
  • Suspension and brake components, body plastics

We confirm prices and availability individually.

HUS

HUS makes electric off-road motorcycles for kids. Our range includes the HUS V2 PRO, HUS V2, HUS Epro V1 16" and HUS Epro V1 12".

  • Batteries and chargers
  • Tyres, inner tubes and wheels
  • Brake components and wear parts

We confirm prices and availability individually.
