Frequently asked questions

Are electric quads suitable for the forest?

Technically yes: an electric off-road quad is quiet, burns no fuel and copes well with roots. Legally, however, the Polish Forest Act (Art. 29) restricts motor vehicles in forests to public roads and signposted forest roads, and an electric quad is a motor vehicle. Ride on your own land, on tracks and on land where you have the owner’s permission. More in the guide Riding legally in the forest.

How long does it take to charge the battery?

Depending on capacity and charger, about 2 to 8 hours from a 230 V socket. Most models in our range can be charged overnight, and some adult motorcycles support a faster charger that shortens breaks during a day at the track.

Can I see the bike before buying?

Yes. Book a presentation and a test ride on closed grounds by phone or e-mail. We will also advise on size, power and suspension settings for your weight.
