Description

Antelopower motorcycles pose a huge challenge to the competition in terms of value for money. For under €7k you get a mid-sized competition motorcycle ready to race in hard enduro events straight out of the box.

Key advantages

  • The best value for money – a full-size, professional machine for hardcore mountain crossings.
  • Massive torque 800–1000 Nm at the wheel.
  • Long wheelbase and huge ground clearance.
  • High-end GFe suspension with components made in Germany; 240 mm travel, optimal for hard enduro, a low centre of gravity and a relatively low step-over height.
  • Quick-release battery 4320 Wh.
  • High-quality four-piston hydraulic brakes.
  • Professional grips and throttle – power delivery is incredibly silky, so the motorcycle is easy to control despite its huge power.
  • Four riding modes, each with three programmes.
  • Display in a steel housing – practically indestructible.
  • Very long, grippy and soft seat.
  • Very soft compound tyres, with the option of fitting thicker tyres.
  • Lightweight – motorcycle weight: 93 kg.
  • Fast acceleration – 0–50 km/h in 1.7 s.
