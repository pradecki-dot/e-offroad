Description

The Arctic Leopard XF PRO L1E is a lightweight electric off-road motorcycle with L1e-B moped road approval (B or AM driving licence). Its 58 kg weight makes it easy to control in tight turns, on jumps and over obstacles.

The 12 kW peak motor delivers 342 Nm of torque at the rear wheel and instant throttle response. Drive is transmitted directly from the motor shaft.

KKE suspension – a 35 mm USD fork with up to 200 mm of travel at the front and an adjustable rear shock – plus 19" front and 16" rear wheels with knobbly off-road tyres provide traction and precise handling. The gold KKE fork and shock come as standard.

The TFT display with NFC and the app let you adjust regenerative braking strength and power delivery, among other things.

In the road-approved version, on public roads, power is 4 kW and top speed 45 km/h. Once unlocked, exclusively off public roads, the motorcycle reaches up to 96 km/h – it is then not road-legal.
