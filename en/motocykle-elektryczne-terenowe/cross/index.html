Electric motocross bikes

An electric motocross bike is built for track riding: jumps, whoops, berms and short, very intense sessions. Compared with an enduro it has stiffer suspension, a more aggressive power curve and no lights, but it is less forgiving on forest roots. Its big advantage is silence — more and more tracks close to houses let electric machines ride at times when combustion bikes have to stay parked.

What to look for when choosing a motocross bike

  • Ride time on the track is shorter than the catalogue range — count on 30–45 minutes of hard riding per charge.
  • The suspension should match your weight; we swap the springs before shipping.
  • Check the track rules: some venues require a transponder and race number from electric motorcycles too.
  • Seat height matters at the start gate — try the motorcycle for size before you buy.
