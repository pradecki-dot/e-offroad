Description

The HUS Epro V1 12" is the smallest HUS electric motorcycle for the youngest riders, with an aluminium frame and 12-inch wheels.

Key features:

  • 12-inch tyres: the smaller size suits younger children better and gives a more comfortable ride.
  • Lightweight disc brake: hydraulic rear brake. The levers are shaped for children’s hands and have a comfortable non-slip grip, while durable metal pads and a sealed, dustproof design ensure consistent braking.
  • Child-sized frame: designed around a child’s body proportions, it reduces fatigue on longer rides and gives stability when rolling and turning.
  • One-piece seat: made of flexible vacuum-formed PU foam. The soft, rounded surface absorbs vibrations.

The motorcycle is intended for riding exclusively off public roads, with a helmet and full protective gear, under adult supervision.
