Description

The HUS Epro V1 16" is a lightweight electric motorcycle for kids with an aluminium frame, hydraulic disc brakes and off-road tyres on 16-inch wheels.

Key features:

  • Wear-resistant off-road tyres: good grip and protection against slipping on grass and gravel, so your child rides confidently in all conditions.
  • Lightweight disc brakes: the levers are shaped for children’s hands and have a comfortable non-slip grip, while durable metal pads and a sealed, dustproof design ensure consistent braking even in tough conditions.
  • Child-sized frame: designed around a child’s body proportions, it reduces fatigue on longer rides and gives stability when rolling and turning.
  • One-piece seat: made of flexible vacuum-formed PU foam. The soft, rounded surface absorbs vibrations.
  • Two speed ranges and Competition mode: a button to select high or low speed, and up to 33 km/h in Competition mode. Plus a battery level indicator and a magnetic safety cut-off switch.

The motorcycle is intended for riding exclusively off public roads, with a helmet and full protective gear, under adult supervision.
