Description

The HUS V2 PRO is a high-quality, ultralight electric motorcycle for kids. HUS motorcycles were created in response to customers asking for a very light, well-engineered electric motorcycle for young riders. It has a light aluminium frame, FastAce competition suspension and a quick-swap battery.

What sets the PRO version apart:

  • Four-piston hydraulic brakes: powerful yet smooth braking with linear force, making stops easy to control. Balanced piston pressure improves riding safety.
  • Upside-down fork with double triple clamp: even suspension rebound gives a more stable ride and more confident cornering, and the robust design handles rough terrain well.
  • Rear shock with 14-step adjustment: set it to suit the conditions and your child’s weight. It soaks up bumps and keeps the bike stable in corners.
  • Colour HD LCD display: shows the key ride data clearly and without lag, and the anti-glare coating makes it easy to read in sunlight.

The motorcycle is intended for riding exclusively off public roads, with a helmet and full protective gear, under adult supervision.
