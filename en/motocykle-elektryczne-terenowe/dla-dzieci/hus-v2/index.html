Description

The HUS V2 is a high-quality, ultralight electric motorcycle for kids. HUS motorcycles were created in response to customers asking for a very light, well-engineered electric motorcycle for children. They have a light aluminium frame, FastAce suspension, hydraulic disc brakes and very fast battery swapping.

Key features:

  • Off-road suspension: even rebound gives a more stable ride and more confident cornering, and the robust design handles rough terrain well.
  • Matched front fork: designed for children’s needs, it noticeably softens bumps and improves cornering.
  • Off-road tyres: wear-resistant, with good grip on grass and gravel.
  • Simple display: three gears and neutral, plus a five-segment battery indicator (each segment means 20%). Each gear has its own backlight colour.
  • Quick battery swap: just open the cover and slide the battery out.

The motorcycle is intended for riding exclusively off public roads, with a helmet and full protective gear, under adult supervision.
