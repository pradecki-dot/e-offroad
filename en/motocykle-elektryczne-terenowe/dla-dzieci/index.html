Electric motorcycles for kids

An electric motorcycle for kids is a great way to learn balance, throttle and braking in calm conditions: nothing gets hot, it’s quiet and there’s no clutch. Safety comes down to three things — a model that matches the child’s height, a speed limiter set by the parent and full protective gear on every ride.

Type approval and road law in Poland

A kids’ electric motocross bike, a pit bike or an electric enduro pit bike is sports equipment intended exclusively for riding off public roads. It has no type approval or number plates, so it must not be ridden on streets, pavements or dirt roads open to traffic. To use roads, a vehicle must be type-approved and registered, have third-party liability insurance, and the rider must hold the appropriate licence (e.g. category AM from age 14 for a moped). Restrictions also apply to forests. Regulations change, so check the current legal situation before riding and always obtain the landowner’s permission.

FAQ — motorcycles for kids

From what age can a child ride an electric motorcycle?

The simplest 12V models with stabilisers are designed for children from about 3 years old. Electric pit bikes for kids usually start at 6, and more powerful 48V models at about 9. More important than age is height (feet on the ground), coordination and whether the child follows instructions.

Is a helmet needed?

Yes, always. Choose a full-face helmet with ECE 22.06 approval in the right size, plus gloves, goggles, knee and elbow pads and boots that cover the ankle. For more powerful models we also recommend a chest protector or body armour.

Where can you ride legally?

On private land with the owner’s permission, on motocross tracks and in off-road parks that accept children. Riding on public roads, pavements or in the forest outside permitted roads is not allowed — kids’ motorcycles are not registered, and the Polish Forest Act prohibits motor vehicles from entering forests.
