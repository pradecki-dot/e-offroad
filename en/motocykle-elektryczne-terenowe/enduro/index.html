Electric enduro and hard enduro motorcycles

An e-enduro is a full-size electric off-road motorcycle built for long loops through forests, gravel, meadows and sandy tracks, not just short sessions at the track. It suits adults who ride for fun or train for competitions and want a machine that is quiet, light to handle and doesn’t leave exhaust fumes in the garage. An electric enduro for adults feels at home in mixed terrain: on roots, in ruts and on moderate climbs.

Technical specification: e-offroad Enduro 8000

  • Motor: 8 kW (16 kW peak), chain drive, three power maps
  • Battery: 72 V / 55 Ah, charging 0–100% in approx. 4.5 h
  • Range: 70–100 km depending on terrain and mode
  • Suspension: USD 270 mm / mono 280 mm, compression and rebound adjustment
  • Weight: 98 kg, seat height 920 mm
  • Top speed: 95 km/h
  • Permitted age: 18+, payload up to 120 kg
  • 21/18-inch wheels, 4-piston disc brakes

Price: PLN 34,900

Electric hard enduro

Hard enduro is the toughest form of off-road riding: rocky gullies, wet roots, steep climbs and obstacles tackled at walking pace. What counts is traction control on the edge of slipping, low weight and precise power delivery — not top speed.

Electric drive suits this discipline exceptionally well. No clutch means no stalling halfway up an obstacle, torque from zero lets you “climb” a step without a run-up, and crawl mode makes it easier to bring the bike back down. The limitation is ride time, but in hard enduro a typical training session lasts 2–3 hours anyway.
