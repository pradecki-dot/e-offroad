EWATT Beast-M

65 kW, 1200 Nm, 150 km/h, range 150 km

Made to order – approx. 2 months

€9,388

← Back to category: Electric motorcycles

Description

EWATT is a well-known and respected manufacturer of tuning batteries and controllers for well-known electric motorcycle brands. Their Beast-M motorcycle competes directly with top-quality full-size motocross bikes such as the Stark Varg or Radian EXR, at a much lower price (a difference of nearly €4,500!). True to its name, the Beast-M offers truly monstrous performance (85 hp and 1200 Nm!) that will satisfy the craziest, most hardcore riders on the planet. Yet the ability to set any power and top-speed settings also makes it a suitable machine for beginners, especially given its weight of 116 kg — low for a full-size motocross bike.

E-OffRoad Sp. z o.o. is the official distributor and importer of the EWATT brand in Poland.
