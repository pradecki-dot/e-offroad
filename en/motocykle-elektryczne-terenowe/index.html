Off-road, you feel the difference from the very first metre. An off-road e-motorcycle has no clutch or gearbox, so you can focus entirely on your line, and full torque is available from zero, which helps on slippery climbs. It needs no warming up, doesn’t flood after a crash and doesn’t need an oil change every few engine hours. Range and charging time remain the limitation, which is why we match battery capacity to how long you actually ride.

Service and parts

Every motorcycle is shipped after a pre-delivery inspection: we tighten the spokes, check chain tension and update the controller software. We stock brake pads, sprockets, inner tubes and throttles, so common parts ship within one working day. Periodic servicing covers the suspension, bearings, a battery capacity test and controller fault diagnostics.

Why buy from us rather than on a marketplace

On a marketplace you buy a parcel; from us you buy a machine with support. We know every model in our range because we ride them ourselves, and we’ll tell you straight if something cheaper suits your terrain better. Documents, warranty and spare parts are all in one place, without hunting for a seller who vanished after the season. Your questions are answered by a person who knows the difference between static sag and rider sag.
