Family business

About us — family-run, with a passion for off-road

e-offroad.com is a family business. We work with off-road vehicles out of passion — and it’s that passion, not a spreadsheet, that decides what goes into our range.

A family united by the outdoors

We share a love of riding where the tarmac ends. We know how much joy a first solo ride across a meadow or along a forest track brings — and how important it is for the machine to fit the rider, not the other way round.

That’s why we treat every question as if it came from one of our own family: calmly, thoroughly and without rushing.

Our showroom

Wood underfoot, mountains on the wall and gear you can touch — take a look inside our showroom, where you can see the vehicles up close.

How we work

We advise you like family

Before we recommend a model, we ask who will ride, where and how often. We’d rather talk you out of a purchase than sell you something that will sit in the garage.

A passion that became a job

Off-roaders are our hobby, so we’re happy to keep talking after you buy — about first rides, suspension settings and battery care.

Family-run means close

We’re not an anonymous platform. Behind every reply to an e-mail or phone call is one of us, who knows our range inside out.

Family off-road

Snow, meadow or forest track — the whole family rides, from the little one in their first helmet to the grown-ups on electric mountain bikes.

Let’s talk

Write or call — we’ll be happy to help you choose a vehicle for yourself or someone in your family.
