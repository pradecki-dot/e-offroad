Electric motorcycle range in practice

e-offroad.com · 15 November 2026

Catalogue range is a starting point, not a promise. Off-road, on one charge you’ll usually cover between half and three quarters of the figure given by the manufacturer.

Where catalogue figures come from

Manufacturers measure range at a constant, moderate speed, on firm ground and with a light rider. That’s handy for comparing models, but far from riding in the forest.

What eats into range off-road

Sand, mud, steep climbs and frequent acceleration use the most energy. The rider’s weight with a backpack, tyre pressure and power mode also matter.

In winter and in mud

At temperatures close to freezing, a lithium battery delivers noticeably less energy. Store it somewhere warm and charge it at room temperature, and expect a shorter distance on the trail.

How to ride for longer

Ride in eco mode on link sections, use regenerative braking on descents, and keep the correct tyre pressure and chain tension. For longer trips, consider a second battery or a faster charger.
