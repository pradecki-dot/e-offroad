Electric work ATVs

An electric work ATV is a tool, not a toy: it carries feed, hay and tools, pulls a trailer of firewood, spreads fertiliser in the orchard and runs along the electric fence. With no exhaust fumes you can drive it into barns, stables and greenhouses, and the quiet motor doesn’t startle animals. In this class, rack capacity, towing capacity, 4×4 drive and a winch are what count.

Registration and road use

Our sample ATVs are work machines for private land and are not road-approved. Driving on a public road, even a short stretch between fields, requires a type-approved and registered vehicle (e.g. as an agricultural tractor or an L7e vehicle), with third-party insurance and a licensed driver. If you need this, ask us before buying — we’ll advise what documents the vehicle should have.
