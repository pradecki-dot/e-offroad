KANDI T9 — 20 kW electric 4x4 buggy

KANDI T9 electric 4x4 buggy: two 10 kW AC motors (front + rear), 15.36 kWh LiFePO4 battery, up to 80 km range, approx. 80 km/h, independent suspension and hydraulic disc brakes.

Description

The KANDI T9 is a full-size electric off-road buggy with four-wheel drive. Two independent AC motors – 10 kW each on the front and rear axle – with vector control deliver approx. 20 kW in total, with power transmitted by drive shafts. The result? Instant torque and sure-footed traction on sand, mud and loose gravel – with no noise and no exhaust fumes.

Power comes from a 15.36 kWh lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) battery built from 100 Ah LFP cells. LiFePO4 technology is known for its durability and safe operation. The manufacturer quotes a range of up to 80 km, a top speed of approx. 80 km/h and a charging time of approx. 8 hours.

A tubular steel frame with an extensive roll cage protects the crew, while independent suspension and hydraulic disc brakes keep you in control on demanding terrain. Comfort comes from electric power steering (EPS), electric start, a digital display in the cockpit and two bucket seats with seat belts.

The model is available in white, red, black and silver (other colours to order), with 14" or 18" wheels depending on the version. We import the vehicle to order directly from the manufacturer – ask us by e-mail or phone about the price, lead time and availability of the 4-seat version.

The KANDI T9 is an off-road vehicle intended for use away from public roads. Specifications are given according to manufacturer data and may vary by version.
