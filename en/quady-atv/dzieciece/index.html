Young riders — safety and age

  • Match power to age and height: 350 W for children aged 4–7, 800 W for children aged 8–12 — always under adult supervision.
  • The parent sets the speed limiter; start with the lowest mode.
  • An approved helmet, gloves, goggles and pads are mandatory, plus boots that cover the ankle.
  • Ride only on private land with the owner’s permission — kids’ quads are not approved for road use.
  • The manufacturer specifies the maximum rider weight; carrying a passenger is not allowed.

FAQ — quads for kids

From what age can a child ride an electric quad?

The smallest 24 V quads with a top speed of 10 km/h are designed for children from about 4 years old. We recommend 48 V, 800 W models from age 8. Always check that the child can reach the footrests and comfortably hold the handlebar.

How does the parental remote work?

A remote with a range of up to about 30 m lets you cut the drive and stop the quad at any moment. It’s the most effective safeguard in the first weeks of learning, before the child has mastered the brake.

Can a child ride a quad on a dirt road?

No, if the road is open to public traffic — even a farm track or dirt road. Kids’ quads are not road-legal vehicles, so they may only be ridden on private land with the owner’s permission.
