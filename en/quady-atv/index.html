Electric quads and ATVs

An electric quad works in three roles. For leisure, it gives a stable, quiet ride across meadows and farm tracks. On a farm, in an orchard or at a stable it replaces the wheelbarrow and a small tractor, without startling animals or leaving exhaust fumes in buildings. For kids it’s a safer start than a motorcycle, because four wheels forgive more mistakes. As an electric quad shop, we test our machines in each of these roles before they go into our range.

Quad or ATV — how we divide our range

In everyday language, quad and ATV mean the same thing: a four-wheeled off-road vehicle with motorcycle-style handlebars. In our shop, “quads” are leisure and sport machines where riding fun and suspension matter, while “ATVs” are workhorses, judged above all by payload, towing capacity and 4×4 drive.

Delivery and assembly

Buying an electric off-road quad online from us doesn’t mean assembling it from instructions. We ship quads and ATVs on a pallet via a freight company, delivered into your garage. Before shipping we fit the handlebar, wheels and mirrors, charge the battery and check the brakes, so once unpacked all you need to do is put on your helmet. On request we deliver with our own transport and give a short operating briefing.
