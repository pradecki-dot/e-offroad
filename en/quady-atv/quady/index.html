Electric off-road quads

A leisure electric quad for adults is the simplest way to ride quietly across meadows, gravel and your own land — with no gear changes and no noise to bother the neighbours. We match power to the rider’s weight and the lie of the land: 1000 W is enough for flat meadows, but for climbs and a heavier rider it’s better to have something in reserve. Every model in this subcategory is an electric quad for off-road riding for pleasure — for heavy work we recommend an ATV.
