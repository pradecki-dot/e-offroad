Electric e-MTB and off-road bikes

An e-MTB combines pedalling effort with motor assistance, so the climb that would normally end a ride becomes just another section of the trail. It’s a good choice for people who want to ride off-road with electric power but without the formalities that come with motor vehicles.

Under Polish law, a bike with assistance that works only while pedalling, up to 25 km/h and with a motor rated at up to 250 W, is treated as a bicycle. It needs no registration or driving licence. All our sample models fall within these limits; rules for riding on trails and forest roads may vary locally, so check the signs and the forest district’s regulations.
